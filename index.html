About Auraffah

Fashion Born from
Faith & Purpose

Auraffah was born from a simple belief — that Muslim men and women deserve clothing that is both modern and meaningful. Every piece we create blends the richness of Islamic tradition with contemporary streetwear and formal fashion.

From hand-crafted calligraphy tees to premium down shoulder shirts, kurtas, trousers, and accessories — garments that let you wear your faith with pride and confidence.
